# Nelson Melina

Nineteen years of software, ten in crypto:

- **Altworth** (2025–) — founding spec to a crypto-native alpha, then a from-scratch fiat rebuild by the three-person core team with AI coding agents (Claude Code, Codex); live since July 2026. The contribution graph below is that.
- **Cork Protocol** (2024–25) — head of engineering from inception to Ethereum mainnet (March 2025), $16M TVL in the first month.
- **paywith.glass** (2022–24) — product owner for cross-border payment rails built to central-bank requirements, including the build for the Hong Kong Monetary Authority's first central bank digital currency pilot (e-HKD). I owned the product, not those relationships.
- Before that: Bitcoin wallet work at Mycelium (500k+ Android installs), an SEC-regulated security-token platform and an invoice-factoring platform as a consultant, contactless payments on Android to Visa's requirements.

Most of the work is in private repos. Public here: [nadi](https://github.com/computerpoet/nadi), an iOS pranayama-and-sit timer, and [pglite-vfs-repro](https://github.com/computerpoet/pglite-vfs-repro), a minimal bug reproduction.
